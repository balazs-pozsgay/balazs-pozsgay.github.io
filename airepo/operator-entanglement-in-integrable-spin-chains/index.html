OE-1. Square-root law for continuous time evolution

Status: Human guided draft

Date added: 2026-11-18

Description: We prove the square root law for the growth of operator entanglement in a special integrable Hamiltonian. Our earlier work proved the square root law only for quantum circuit, where the gate is a permutation gate in a given basis (see arXiv:2609.39401). The current document proves the presence of the square root law in systems which are genuinely quantum mechanical.
