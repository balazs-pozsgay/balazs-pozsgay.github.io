Project:

Boundary modes, strong zero modes

Description of the project

We consider spin chain models with local Hamiltonian densities on a half-infinite chain. We ask when these models admit quasi-local operators that are localized near the boundary and commute with the Hamiltonian. We are interested in both strong zero modes and other conserved modes. For which types of models do such operators exist, and how can they be constructed?
